Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page explains what information OPTIVERSE X METHOD ("we", "the site") keeps about you and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run your account.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Password: only as a salted, one-way hash. We cannot read your password.
  • Plan and activity: your plan (Free, Pro or Ultra), the card code you activated and the time of your last login.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To create your account and log you in.
  • To send you one-time email codes that confirm it is really you.
  • To apply your plan and keep your account secure.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies: one keeps you logged in, and one remembers a trusted device so we do not ask for a code every time. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Your data is stored in a private storage area that only the site owner can access. Email codes are sent through an email provider, and Google handles the Google sign-in step.

Your choices

You can log out of every device from your account page and change your password at any time. To ask us to delete your account or correct your details, contact us through the Support page on the site.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
